Live Drops: Black Friday and Christmas

Two flash-sale moments on TikTok Live. Live-only prices and a lucky draw for three iPads gave people a reason to tune in on Black Friday; branded merchandise did the same at Christmas. I ran the preparation, from listings to delivery.

The brief

Stand out when every brand is on sale

The goal

Sales in the two biggest weeks of the year

Black Friday and Christmas, through TikTok Live.

The problem

Discounts alone don't stand out

In peak season everyone cuts prices. Viewers needed a reason to watch our live, and to buy during it.

The answer

Give the live its own reason to exist

Better prices only on the live, plus a prize worth staying for.

Two moments

One hook for each occasion

Black Friday

Live-only prices and a lucky draw

  • Better prices during the live stream than anywhere else
  • Buying a selected item entered the customer into a lucky draw
  • Three winners each received an iPad
Why it works

The draw was tied to specific items, so it steered demand to the products we chose to push, not just to the cheapest ones.

Christmas

Branded merchandise across categories

  • Brand items, including hoodies, bags and mugs
  • Matched to different product categories
  • A gift-season reason to buy before Christmas
Why it works

Branded items keep the brand in the home long after the appliance is unboxed — given as a gift with qualifying purchases, not sold separately.

What I'd do differently

The live is the last step, not the first

Warm up the audience earlier

Short teaser videos in the days before, so viewers arrive already knowing about the draw.

Show the draw in public

Pick winners on camera and announce them, so the prize builds trust as well as excitement.

Turn follow-up into reviews

The on-time delivery check is the right moment to ask for a review.

What this campaign shows

A good offer only works if the operation behind it does
