Live Drops: Black Friday and Christmas

Two flash-sale moments on TikTok Live. Live-only prices and a lucky draw for three iPads gave people a reason to tune in on Black Friday; branded merchandise — a hoodie, a water bottle, a backpack and a T-shirt — did the same at Christmas. I ran the preparation, from listings to delivery.

The brief

Stand out when every brand is on sale

The goal

Sales in the two biggest weeks of the year

Black Friday and Christmas, through TikTok Live.

The problem

Discounts alone don't stand out

In peak season everyone cuts prices. Viewers needed a reason to watch our live, and to buy during it.

The answer

Give the live its own reason to exist

Better prices only on the live, plus a prize worth staying for.

The live streams sat alongside the brand's always-on marketplace sales on Temu and AliExpress, so each channel had a different job.

Three channels

Three channels, three jobs

Demand

TikTok Live

Creates demand. Content and entertainment give people a reason to watch and buy. Streams were hosted by TikTok creators and by the brand's own hosts.

Highest daily sales

Temu

The channel with the highest daily sales. My work was product placement and negotiating platform price subsidies.

Same approach, smaller scale

AliExpress

Roughly a fifth of Temu's volume, run on the same approach: product placement and price-subsidy negotiation with the platform.

Negotiation

Negotiating both sides

With the brand

Making the case for extra subsidy

Using monthly sales by model, I made the case for extra subsidies on the best-selling models, to push their volume further.

With the platforms

Traffic support

I secured traffic vouchers and, on TikTok, live-stream traffic support. With that support, live views rose 40% and sales rose 10%.

Two moments

One hook for each occasion

Black Friday

Live-only prices and a lucky draw

  • Better prices during the live stream than anywhere else
  • Buying a selected item entered the customer into a lucky draw
  • Three winners each received an iPad
  • Winners were confirmed live during the stream
Why it works

The draw was tied to specific items, so it steered demand to the products we chose to push, not just to the cheapest ones.

Christmas

Branded merchandise across categories

  • Brand items: a hoodie, a water bottle, a backpack and a T-shirt
  • Matched to different product categories
  • A gift-season reason to buy before Christmas
Why it works

Branded items keep the brand in the home long after the appliance is unboxed — given as a gift with qualifying purchases, not sold separately.

Creative

Brainstormed together, produced by the brand

Teaser videos and short-form content were brainstormed together with the brand's team; the brand produced them.

What I'd do differently

The live is the last step, not the first

Warm up the audience earlier

Short teaser videos in the days before, so viewers arrive already knowing about the draw.

Turn follow-up into reviews

The on-time delivery check is the right moment to ask for a review.

Beyond the UK

Germany and the Netherlands

I also worked with clients in Germany and the Netherlands, selling through Amazon, OTTO and the brands' own websites. The approach was similar to Temu and AliExpress — product selection and placement — but the range was adapted to each platform's own audience rather than copied across.
