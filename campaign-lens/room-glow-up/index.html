Campaign case study · UNINN Student Accommodation · 2022

Room Glow-Up Challenge

Decorate your room. Post it. Win. We invited residents to style their rooms and share them on Xiaohongshu, TikTok and Instagram. The three most-liked rooms won, and the top prize was a Hermès wallet.

The brief

Let residents tell the story for us

The goal

Real content from real residents

Posts made by the people who actually live here, reaching their own followers, not only ours.

The insight

Students already decorate, and already post

A styled room is something students are proud of. We gave them a reason to share it with us.

The audience

Residents, and the students they reach

Every entry was also a room tour for students choosing where to live next year.

How it worked

Three steps, no barrier to entry

Decorate

Residents styled their own room, in their own way.

Post

Share it on Xiaohongshu, TikTok or Instagram, tagging UNINN with @liveintheUK @myroom @decrorateroom @studenthousing @beautifulroom.

Collect likes

Every like counted. Entrants rallied their friends, which spread the posts further.

Top three win

The three most-liked rooms won a prize. Entries ran 1 November – 1 December 2023.

What I'd do differently

Make every entry work twice

Reward more than likes

Likes favour entrants with big followings. I'd add a judges' choice, so creativity counts as well as reach.

Reuse the best rooms

With permission, winning rooms become ready-made content for the next booking season.

Set clear terms up front

Entry dates, how winners are chosen, and consent to reuse content, published before launch.
