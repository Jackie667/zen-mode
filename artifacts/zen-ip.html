The Story

Where It Comes From

The character is a meditation figure inside an ensō circle — the Zen Buddhist symbol of wholeness, emptiness, and the imperfect nature of all things. It's hand-drawn with brush strokes on purpose: nothing is perfectly symmetrical, and that's the point. The imperfection is the personality.

Every product in this range carries that same philosophy — made to be held, used, and worn without being precious about it. A mug that gets coffee rings. A badge that gets scuffed. A card that bends in your wallet. Things that live with you, not above you.
